Ski Tracker · redesign, run 1 · mockup v2 · nothing changed in the app

Record, in alpine dawn

Ski Tracker becomes an instrument, not a feed. Snow-white paper, warm graphite ink, and one glacier-ice accent that appears only when something is live or earned. The numbers carry the screen: large, light and tabular, with small quiet labels, like a chronograph dial. Boxes give way to hairlines and space, and glass is kept for floating chrome. Every control is sized for a gloved hand and every figure for direct sun.

Moodboard: fresh corduroy at first light. The engraved back of a steel watch case. Undyed wool and pale oak. A printed piste map, folded once. Frost on a window, not neon on a slope.

Seven-point check

The seven fixes from the video, and what each one changed in this version.

  • 1 · Focal pointIdle: Start is the only solid control and the only thing that moves, and the resort name drops from 49 to 25. Recording: the live figure is the only high-contrast element, because Pause and Finish became tonal.
  • 2 · SpacingEvery gap is 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 or 48. Related items sit 4 to 8 apart, groups 24 apart. The mockup build fails if a gap is off the scale.
  • 3 · TypographySix sizes instead of thirteen: 11, 13, 16, 20, 25 and the 120 hero. Hierarchy comes from weight and colour: headings 25 Medium, figures 25 Regular, support text muted.
  • 4 · ColourWarm neutrals everywhere. Glacier marks only what is live, selected or primary: the Start ring, the state dot, the selected metric, the top-speed flash. Red only on SOS.
  • 5 · ContentA real resort, lift and piste name, no placeholders. The numbers now agree with each other: run time, distance, top speed, vertical, altitude and the battery estimate.
  • 6 · DetailsOne radius, 24, plus pills. Buttons and the tab bar sit concentric with the screen corner. Hairlines are 1 px. Shadows are two soft layers, on floating chrome only. Every icon has the same 1.5 pt stroke.
  • 7 · MotionMotion starts where you act: the metric underline slides from the label you tap, Finish fills from the left while you hold, the sheet follows your finger and settles. Exits take half the time of entrances. Everything eases out.

For your review

Choices in this mockup that differ from the brief, the video or the app today.

  1. The live figure is 120 pt Light and shrinks to 96 for long values. The brief says 56 to 96.
  2. While recording there is no solid button. Finish is tonal and needs a one-second hold. Start is the only solid control in the tab.
  3. Sport moved from a chip row into a list row. Changing sport now takes two taps instead of one.
  4. The greys stay warm, as your brief decided. The video would tint them towards the accent colour instead.
  5. Tab bar keeps small labels. Icon-only is a one-line change.
  6. The eight stat tiles become three figures, two rows and an “All figures” sheet. Nothing is removed.
  7. Not drawn yet: fall check card, unfinished-day banner, jump callout, challenge finish card, and the high-contrast option.
  8. Mürren, Schiltgrat and Kandahar are real names, but the figures are demo data and are not checked against the real piste map.
  9. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Offline, this page falls back to the system font.
